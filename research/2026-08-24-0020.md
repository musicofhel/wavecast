## Findings / surprises
- **The single biggest lever found so far in Phase B is trivial: stop flipping every bar.** Daily mean-reversion with a 5-day holding period goes from negative to +0.30 mean OOS Sharpe (train agrees, +0.16 vs negative at hold 1), purely by cutting turnover ~4x.
- Persistence never becomes positive at any hold — the daily edge is genuinely mean-reversion-shaped, not trend-shaped.
- Vol-scaled sizing ≈ neutral vs flat (+0.31 vs +0.30): no measurable benefit at this granularity.
- Honesty caveats: +0.30 is a cross-sectional mean with only 11–12/20 tickers positive — it is not a tradeable system claim, and no multiple-comparison correction has been applied across the 20 configs tried. It should be read as "holding periods rescue daily mean-reversion from cost death", not as alpha confirmation.
- Pyramiding and exit rules (stop/target) were NOT implemented this pass — held back to keep the diff small; the holding-period result suggests they're the next marginal question, not the binding one.
