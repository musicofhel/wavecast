## Findings / surprises
- **The A4 "persistence Sharpe +2.18" does not transfer to per-bar hourly trading.** At 7bps round-trip with a position flip every bar, hourly persistence is −0.85 median across the universe — costs dominate. A4's number came from daily-resolved forward predictions (one decision/day), not bar-by-bar flipping. Aaron's question ("should Phase B prioritize persistence?") gets a nuanced answer: persistence-like structure may exist but ONLY at low turnover (daily or with holding periods); B4's holding-period/exit variants are the right place to test it, not raw bar-flipping.
- Hourly is broadly cost-hostile (median −0.95 vs daily −0.27); anything hourly needs either sub-7bps costs, holding periods, or a real per-bar edge to clear costs.
- Per-bar sign rules have ~99% turnover (flat rate ~1% = only the seed bar), so these numbers are essentially pure-cost floors — useful as the harness's calibration reference.
