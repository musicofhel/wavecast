## Findings / surprises
1. **Massive 429 rate limiting is the dominant operational failure.** Even at
   pacing_seconds=0.5–1.0, multi-ticker cycles lose 10–30% of tickers to
   "too many 429 error responses" after the built-in backoff (max 16s). This
   is what killed the production cron on JPM per the A2 report; it is still
   live. A longer exponential backoff or per-key pacing would raise coverage.
   (aaron-gated if it needs a plan change.)
2. `CatchupRunner.run_for_session()` logs predictions but never resolves them —
   resolution had to be a separate explicit phase (phase 3 in the script).
   Worth a follow-up task if backfilled ledgers should feed eval directly.
3. Session-end predictions (bar 23:00, target midnight) resolve via the
   fallback path and can produce actual_return exactly 0.0 — a small
   systematic artifact for the last hourly bar of each catch-up session.
