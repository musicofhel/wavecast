## Findings / surprises
1. **B4's headline does not survive honest evaluation.** hold=5 (+0.30 peeked)
   becomes −0.14 on these folds and walk-forward selection of it yields −0.20.
   The h5 result was a same-window selection artifact.
2. **hold is regime-dependent, not a fixed constant**: selection scattered
   across all five candidates; 9/20 tickers switch hold between folds.
3. **h=10's +0.83 is itself peeked** — best of five holds examined on these
   folds, so it carries a multiple-comparison premium. It suggests longer
   holding keeps helping (cost amortization dominates), but the only
   unpeeked number in this pass is walk-forward −0.20.
4. Window caveat vs B4: this pass uses train_bars=1000 expanding with folds
   over the remainder (~last ~1.5y of each series), not B4's 2024-06-30 split;
   part of the discrepancy is region, but the selection-rate result (a) is
   window-independent evidence of instability.
